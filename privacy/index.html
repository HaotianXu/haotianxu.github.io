Privacy

How this website uses basic analytics and how to opt out.

This website uses PostHog to understand how often its pages are visited and the approximate countries and cities visitors come from. Analytics reports are private and are not displayed on the website.

PostHog uses an IP address to estimate location, then discards the raw IP address before storing the analytics event. A randomly generated browser identifier is stored in local storage to estimate repeat visits. This identifier does not tell me your name or email address.

The site records page paths and the referring website’s domain. URL query strings, fragments, and referrer paths are removed. Session recordings, click tracking, heatmaps, and console-log capture are disabled. Location and visitor counts are approximate.

Your choice

You can turn off analytics for this browser using the button below. The site also respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. This privacy page does not send analytics events.

Your browser preference applies to future visits. Clearing your browser storage also clears this preference.
